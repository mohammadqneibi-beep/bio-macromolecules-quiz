Metabolism in one slide

الأيض: مجموع التفاعلات الكيميائية في الخلية

  • Metabolism = all chemical reactions in an organism.
  • Catabolic pathways break molecules down and release energy (e.g., cellular respiration).
  • Anabolic pathways build complex molecules and consume energy (e.g., protein synthesis).
  • Almost every step is catalysed by a specific enzyme.
Key idea: without enzymes, most reactions in the body would be far too slow to sustain life.

Activation energy (EA)

طاقة التنشيط

  • Reactants must absorb energy to reach the unstable transition state.
  • Enzymes lower EA, so more molecules react per second.
  • Enzymes do not change ΔG: they cannot make an unfavourable reaction favourable.
  • Enzymes are not consumed and can be reused many times.
Analogy: an enzyme is a tunnel through a mountain — the start and end points stay the same, but the climb is much lower.

Active site & specificity

الموقع النشط وخصوصية الإنزيم

  • Most enzymes are proteins; their 3-D shape creates an active site.
  • The substrate binds by weak interactions (hydrogen bonds, ionic bonds).
  • Induced fit: binding causes the active site to mould more tightly around the substrate.
  • Specificity comes from the shape and chemistry of the active site (e.g., sucrase acts on sucrose, not lactose).
Link to Nadia’s case: lactase deficiency means lactose is not hydrolysed in the small intestine → lactose intolerance.

Catalytic cycle

  • 1. Substrate enters the active site → enzyme–substrate complex.
  • 2. Active site lowers EA: orients substrates, strains bonds, provides a favourable micro-environment, or briefly bonds covalently.
  • 3. Substrate is converted to products.
  • 4. Products are released; the active site is free for a new substrate.
Rate: increases with substrate concentration until all active sites are occupied (saturation).

Enzyme inhibitors

مثبطات الإنزيمات

  • Competitive: resembles the substrate and blocks the active site; overcome by more substrate (e.g., methotrexate vs dihydrofolate reductase).
  • Non-competitive: binds elsewhere and changes the enzyme’s shape.
  • Irreversible: covalent binding (e.g., aspirin acetylates cyclooxygenase; nerve agents inhibit acetylcholinesterase).
  • Allosteric regulation & feedback inhibition switch pathways on and off.

Enzymes in the kitchen

الإنزيمات في المطبخ

  • Bromelain (pineapple) and papain (papaya) tenderise meat by hydrolysing proteins — and stop gelatin from setting.
  • Rennet (chymosin) coagulates milk proteins in cheese-making.
  • Yeast enzymes ferment sugars → CO₂ makes dough rise.
  • Polyphenol oxidase browns cut apples; lemon juice (low pH) and heat slow it.
  • Over-proofing: yeast exhausts sugars and gluten weakens → dough collapses.
