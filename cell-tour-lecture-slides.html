Clinical link: some antibiotics target 70S ribosomes, sparing human 80S ribosomes.

Nucleus & ribosomes

  • Nucleus: double-membrane envelope with pores; contains chromatin and the nucleolus (rRNA synthesis).
  • Ribosomes: make proteins; free ribosomes → cytosolic proteins; bound ribosomes (on rough ER) → secreted and membrane proteins.
Think: why would a pancreatic cell that secretes insulin have lots of rough ER?

The endomembrane system

النظام الغشائي الداخلي

  • Rough ER: protein synthesis and folding; Smooth ER: lipid synthesis, detoxification (liver), Ca²⁺ storage (muscle).
  • Golgi apparatus: modifies, sorts and ships proteins (adds mannose-6-phosphate tags to lysosomal enzymes).
  • Lysosomes: acidic digestive compartments (phagocytosis, autophagy).
  • Vacuoles: storage; the central vacuole of plant cells keeps cells turgid.
Clinical link: Tay-Sachs disease — a missing lysosomal enzyme lets lipids build up in neurons.

Energy organelles & peroxisomes

  • Mitochondria: cellular respiration → ATP; own DNA and ribosomes; very abundant in muscle and heart cells.
  • Chloroplasts (plants): photosynthesis.
  • Peroxisomes: oxidise fatty acids and detoxify; make H₂O₂ and break it down with catalase.
Endosymbiont theory: mitochondria and chloroplasts descend from engulfed prokaryotes.
